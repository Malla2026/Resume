excerpt: 'Nepal has suffered heavily from local and transboundary air pollution, particularly PM2.5.Coal-fired brick production is one of the main sources of air pollution. The government has prioritized the agenda of reducing the air pollution from the brick industry, for which an economic analysis of adopting cleaner production technologies is imperative. This study analyzes the economics of various options to adopt cleaner technologies and reduce coal consumption to lower emissions from Nepal's brick industry.'
