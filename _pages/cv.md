Work experience
======
* 2012 to date: Independent and Short Term Consultant
  * World Bank, Washington, DC, USA
  * Duties includes: Developing and analyzing sustainable energy transition models and policies.  

* July 2020 - August 2020: NRFU Enumerator
  * US Census
   
* 2007-2012: Sr. Researcher
  * NextGen Solutions, Kathmandu, Nepal
  * Duties included: Modeling, assessing and analysing of sectoral-based energy uses in developing countries. Assessment and analysis of sectoral-based energy uses in Kathmandu Valley, Nepal. Selected specific research works include (i) identification and proposition of potential clean development mechanism projects; (ii) assessment of rural energy management; (iii) Renewable energy education awareness, both in urban and rural areas of Nepal.
 
* 2004-2006: Senior Research Specialist and Adjunct Faculty
  * Asian Institute of Technology, Thailand.
  * Duties included: Teaching and working in the area of sustainable energy development and climate policy assessments. Selected specific research works include (i) Integrated assessment modeling for developing countries and analyses of mitigation options and sustainable development opportunities; (ii) sub-regional state of the environment assessment and reporting on UNEP’s global environmental output - 4 (GEO-4); and (iii) analyses of energy systems development and mitigation options of greenhouse gases and local air pollutant emissions in Asian countries.

* 2001-2002: Student Assistant
  * University of Hawaii/East-West Center, Hawaii, USA
  * Duties included: Development of a dataset on the labor force in a knowledge-based economy in the United States and its analysis

* 1998--1999: Consultant
  * Asian Development Bank, Phillipines
  * Duties included: Coordinated national reports and conducted national workshops and regional seminars in Asia involving different governments, donors, academic institutions, the private sector, and non-government organizations. Provided training on RAINS-ASIA model to governmental officials in China, India, Indonesia, and Thailand. Responsible for the establishment and maintenance of Acid Rain in Asia networks to promote the awareness of economic aspects of environmental issues within and beyond Asia and the Pacific region. 

* January 1996 – August 1996: Research Associate
  * United Nations Environmental Program/Regional Resource Center for Asia and the Pacific (UNEP/RRC-AP), Thailand.
  * Duties included: Modeling of fossil-fuel-related acid rain issues in Asia and the Pacific Region. Helped develop air pollution section of the State of the Environment Report for selected Asia and the Pacific countries.
    
* 1993 – 1995: Research Associate
  * Asian Institute of Technology (AIT). ADB and the World Bank-funded project on Acid Rain and Emissions Reduction in Asia - Phase I project, Thailand.
  * Duties included: Prepared acid rain-related data collection and its analysis in Asia. Evaluated the RAINS-ASIA model for the analysis of emission of acid rain precursors, their atmospheric transport, deposition, and impacts. Coordinated national reports and conducted national workshops and regional seminars in Asia.

* 1990 – 1991: Divisional Electrical Engineer
  * Nepal Hydro and Electric Company, Butwal, Nepal
  * Duties included: Development of micro hydro power plants and in rural areas of Nepal
