This user guide introduces the Multi-Tier Framework (MTF) Nepal household energy survey data analysis using Stata. The MTF survey is a global baseline survey on household access to electricity and clean cooking conducted by the [World Bank] (https://www.worldbank.org). Stata is a statistical software for data science developed by [StataCorp LLC] (https://www.stata.com). The main purpose of this guide is to serve as an instruction manual for researchers who are involved in MTF survey data analysis using Stata. It could also be useful for students and government officials interested in learning MTF survey data analysis and statistics.
<br>
The user guide provides step-by-step instructions to analyze MTF survey data and conduct a simple statistical analysis. Based on our over 25 years of experience in teaching and conducting research in the areas of energy, climate, and economy, we have included two main features that we feel are important in learning MTF survey data analysis. First, this user guide provides an introduction to the United Nations (UN) Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), in particular, SDG7, MTF survey data associated with SDG7, and the nature and quality of publicly available relevant government and non-government data in developing countries. Our focus country in the guidebook is Nepal. Understanding of these topics is important before conducting any SDG7-related empirical research in developing countries. Second, the guidebook teaches technical and non-technical users, including students, researchers, and government officials, how to analyze MTF survey data, understand descriptive statistics, and describe statistical results. We believe that understanding the dataset and communicating statistical results using the dataset simply and transparently is key to successful research projects.
<b>   
This guidebook assumes that users have basic statistics knowledge and know basic functionality of Stata software. The guidebook does not teach statistics.
